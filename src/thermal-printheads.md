sectionFiveHeadline: "Signs your thermal printhead needs replacing"

sectionFiveContent: |
 Thermal printheads gradually wear during normal operation. Replacing them before they fail completely helps maintain print quality and reduce production problems. Common signs include:

 - Missing dots or white lines through printed codes
 - Poor barcode readability
 - Uneven print density
 - Smudged or incomplete images
 - Increased ribbon wear
 - Frequent print quality warnings
 - Higher reject rates

 If you’re seeing any of these issues, it may be time to replace your thermal printhead.
